Lumina PMAG™

Polymer Modified
Composite Gypsum

Lumina PMAG™ is Studio Luminant’s core material — a polymer modified composite gypsum formulated for high-specification architectural installations.

More moisture resistant than standard gypsum, and available in any RAL or custom colour. Ideal for large-scale interior applications where surface quality and dimensional accuracy are non-negotiable: walls, ceilings, column wraps, counter fronts and accent surfaces.
