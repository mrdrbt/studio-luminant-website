The pattern

Fioritura gathers the plants of Ottoman tile ornament: tulip buds, rosettes with raised centres and the serrated saz leaf. Winding stems tie them together, so the motifs read as one interlaced network rather than as separate flowers.

The relief reaches 16 mm. The rosette centres and the spines of the leaves stand forward while the ground sits back, and that difference is enough for even the smallest leaf to cast its own shadow.

How it reads in light

The image at the top of the page is a render computed from this design’s depth map; on a computer the light follows your cursor, on a phone it moves as you scroll. With the light at the side, the rosettes catch it like points and the stems are drawn by the shadow between them. In head-on light the shadows shorten and the surface becomes a pale, fine texture; the flowers only separate as you come close.

On the wall

Fioritura is a field layout. Laid side by side, the 600 × 600 mm panels do not break the pattern at their edges: the stems pass from one panel into the next and the wall reads as a single floral field. With a 300 mm repeat it can carry the same texture across a broad wall, a niche or a column face. Where the pattern is cut at the edges of the wall is decided together on the layout drawing.

Where it works

With a repeat every 300 mm, Fioritura can cover a broad wall, a niche or a column face in the same texture. In raking light the rosettes catch it like points and the stems are drawn by the shadow between them. In head-on light the surface turns to a fine, even texture, and the flowers separate only at close range. Pieces come paint-ready or with an artisan finish; a paint-ready surface can be painted on site to match the room.

Frequently asked questions

What size are Fioritura panels, and how are they laid?

Fioritura is designed as a 600 × 600 mm relief wall panel, and the pattern repeats every 300 mm. Laid side by side, the pattern and its stems run on from panel to panel, and the wall reads as a single field of flowers. Where the pattern is cut at the edges of the wall, we agree with you on the layout drawing.

How deep is the Fioritura relief?

The relief reaches 16 mm. The hearts of the rosettes and the spines of the leaves stand forward while the ground falls back, so the leaves cast their own shadows.

What is the lead time for Fioritura, and how is it fixed?

Lead time is 4 weeks after contract and advance for orders up to 50 m²; for larger orders the quote sets the date. Delivery is ex-works Sapanca (EXW). Pieces are hand-cast in Lumina PMAG™ — Polymer Modified Composite Gypsum and fixed to the wall with a polymer adhesive, or with a rail system on metal stud and plasterboard walls. Pieces that wrap a column, turn a corner or follow a curved wall are cast as Bespoke, in the same motif. The minimum order is 5 m². The mould is made for your order and you approve the master before casting; both are included in the 4 weeks.
