In a room

Where it works

Hemiola is built from blocks, each with an opening turned off square, and reads as a wall laid by hand; a 1.5 mm joint runs between the blocks. The renders on this page show it in two places: rising behind the seating of a lobby lounge, and between the dining area and the bar of a restaurant. The relief comes forward in raking light and settles in head-on light. What to settle first on a lobby wall like this is set out in the hotel lobby feature wall guide. Pieces come paint-ready or with an artisan finish; a paint-ready surface can be painted on site to match the room.

Frequently asked questions

What pieces make up Hemiola, and how are they laid?

The wall is laid from blocks cast in four moulds, A to D, with a 1.5 mm joint between them. The letters show where each block sits in the lay drawn on this page. Send the dimensions of your wall and we show how Hemiola sits on it in a layout drawing; the edges of the wall, column faces and outside corners are worked out on the same drawing.

How deep is the Hemiola relief?

The depth shows in the close-up render on this page. The mould is made for your order and you approve the master before anything is cast; the cast pieces match that master, so you see the depth of the relief on it before casting starts.

What is the lead time for Hemiola, and how is it fixed?

Lead time is 4 weeks after contract and advance for orders up to 50 m²; for larger orders the quote sets the date. Delivery is ex-works Sapanca (EXW). Pieces are hand-cast in Lumina PMAG™ — Polymer Modified Composite Gypsum and their fixing is set in the installation drawing for each project. Pieces that wrap a column, turn a corner or follow a curved wall are cast as Bespoke, in the same motif. The minimum order is 5 m². The mould is made for your order and you approve the master before casting; both are included in the 4 weeks.
