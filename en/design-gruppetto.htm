In a room

Where it works

A wall that stretches out horizontally gives Gruppetto’s long S-curves room to run, each one over several pieces. In the concept images on this page it sits on the wall behind a bar counter and beside the small tables of a café, painted terracotta in the first and a pale stone colour in the second. The curves sharpen in raking light and settle in head-on light. Pieces come paint-ready and can be painted on site to match the room.

Frequently asked questions

What pieces make up Gruppetto, and how are they laid?

Gruppetto is a relief wall panel design laid from one fan-shaped form, repeated; the S-curves come from the direction in which the pieces are laid. The drawing on this page shows that lay. Send the dimensions of your wall and we show how Gruppetto sits on it in a layout drawing; the edges of the wall, column faces and outside corners are worked out on the same drawing.

How deep is the Gruppetto relief?

The relief is 30 mm. The mould is made for your order and you approve the master before anything is cast; the cast pieces match that master, so you see the depth of the relief on it before casting starts.

What is the lead time for Gruppetto, and how is it fixed?

Lead time is 4 weeks after contract and advance for orders up to 50 m²; for larger orders the quote sets the date. Delivery is ex-works Sapanca (EXW). Pieces are hand-cast in Lumina PMAG™ — Polymer Modified Composite Gypsum and fixed to the wall with a polymer adhesive, or with a rail system on metal stud and plasterboard walls. Pieces that wrap a column, turn a corner or follow a curved wall are cast as Bespoke, in the same motif. The minimum order is 5 m². The mould is made for your order and you approve the master before casting; both are included in the 4 weeks. The bottom course sits on a fixed ledger and each course stands on the one below; the wall surface is checked before fixing.
