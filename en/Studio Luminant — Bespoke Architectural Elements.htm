Your concept becomes a 3D model first, and you approve the master before anything is cast, so the surface your client approves is the surface we cast. Your design stays yours; we carry the modelling and the casting.

01
Design & Modelling

From Brief to 3D Model

Send a sketch, a reference, or a brief. We turn it into a high-resolution 3D surface model, with the texture and depth the design calls for.

02
Moulding & Casting

Mould Masters, Precision Casting

We make the mould master straight from the model, 3D-printed or CNC-machined depending on the geometry, then pour each panel in Lumina PMAG™ and leave it in the mould to set, carrying every detail of the mould surface into the panel — every batch checked for surface integrity before it leaves the floor.

03
Finishing & Delivery

Artisan Finish and Crating

Each panel is either finished by hand in the workshop in the chosen artisan finish or leaves primed for the site painter, then is crated with its technical data sheet and installation drawing.

What to Know
Before You Specify.

What are your lead times?

Studio Designs orders up to 50 m² are ready for ex-works dispatch from Sapanca 4 weeks after contract and advance; for larger orders the quote sets the date. Bespoke takes 6 weeks at minimum, longer with the project’s scale, the number of moulds and the finish. We give the exact programme in writing in the quote.

Lead Times →
How heavy are the panels — do they need special support?

Weight depends on panel thickness and relief depth; on solid walls panels are fixed with a polymer adhesive, and on metal stud and plasterboard walls with a rail system. Structural data is included in the specification package.

Weight & Structural →
Is there a minimum project size?

Bespoke runs from a single feature wall to full-floor cladding; for very small jobs we assess fit together first. The minimum order for a Studio Design is 5 m², and 7.5 m² on Andante, Mezzavoce, Glissando, Accelerando and Fugato. Below that area, the job is priced as Bespoke.

What do you provide for the specification?

A technical data sheet and an installation drawing for every project; we settle the specification wording with your office.

Specification Guide →
How different is this to specify and install?

Less than you might think. Lumina PMAG™ is not a drop-in for GRG or GRC, which are not interchangeable with each other either, but the detailing will be familiar: at its standard 25 mm, PMAG sits in GRC’s weight class (35–40 kg/m²), so it takes the same substrate checks, and the same kind of rail fixings on metal stud and plasterboard walls. Our specification guide gives you a specification checklist; clauses and fixing details for your project come on request.

Specification Guide →
Do you handle installation?

Every panel ships with installation drawings and details for your contractor. Where you need us on site, we quote installation per project. Panels are supplied EXW Sapanca by default.
