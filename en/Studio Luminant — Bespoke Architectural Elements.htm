How We Work

Brief to Crated Panel,
One Process.

01
Design & Modelling

From Brief to 3D Model

Send a sketch, a reference, or a brief. We turn it into a high-resolution 3D surface model, with the texture and depth the design calls for. Iteration is digital, so changes here cost nothing.

02
Moulding & Casting

Mould Masters, Precision Casting

We make the mould master straight from the model, 3D-printed or CNC-machined depending on the geometry, then pour each panel in Lumina PMAG™ and leave it in the mould to set, carrying every detail of the mould surface into the panel — every batch checked for surface integrity before it leaves the floor.

03
Finishing & Delivery

Artisan Finish and Crating

Each panel is either finished by hand in the workshop in the chosen artisan finish or leaves primed for the site painter, then is crated with its technical data sheet and installation drawing.

The Studio

A workshop,
not a catalogue.

Studio Luminant is a design-led workshop in Sakarya, founded by Burak Tanlak. Design, formulation, mould-making, casting and quality control all happen in our Sapanca studio. Production is run by a team that comes from contract-manufacturing management in this sector. Casting is done in the studio by experienced casting teams under contract, working from our moulds and to our quality control, so casting capacity scales with the job. Packing and installation have their own contracted teams.

The process does not change with the size of the order: every change in writing, a 3D model before anything is cast, and structural data your engineer signs off. Open questions are settled at the model stage.

Frequently Asked

What to Know
Before You Specify.

What are your lead times?

It depends on the project’s scale, the number of moulds and the finish. We give the exact programme, to ex-works dispatch from Sapanca, in writing in the quote.

Lead Times →
How heavy are the panels — do they need special support?

Weight depends on panel thickness and relief depth; most applications use standard mounting systems. Structural data is included in the specification package.

Weight & Structural →
Is there a minimum project size?

Bespoke runs from a single feature wall to full-floor cladding; for very small jobs we assess fit together first. Each Studio Design has its own minimum order area, stated in the quote.

What do you provide for the specification?

A technical data sheet and an installation drawing for every project; we settle the specification wording with your office.

Specification Guide →
How different is this to specify and install?

Less than you might think. Lumina PMAG™ is not a drop-in for GRG or GRC, which are not interchangeable with each other either, but the detailing will be familiar: at its standard 25 mm, PMAG sits in GRC’s weight class (35–40 kg/m²), so it takes the same kind of rail fixings and substrate checks. Our specification guide gives you a specification checklist; clauses and fixing details for your project come on request.

Specification Guide →
Do you handle installation?

Every panel ships with installation drawings and details for your contractor. Where you need us on site, we quote installation per project. Panels are supplied EXW Sapanca by default.
