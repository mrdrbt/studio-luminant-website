Design Freedom,
Without the Specification Gamble.

Bespoke relief shouldn't mean bespoke headaches. Traditional fabricators make you choose between design ambition and programme certainty — intricate geometry is usually the first thing simplified in production.

We close that gap. Your concept becomes a 3D model first, then a physical sample — digital iteration costs nothing, and the surface your client approves is the surface we cast. Your design stays yours; we carry the modelling and the casting.

How We Work

Brief to Installation,
Under One Roof.

One in-house pipeline — design, moulding, casting, finishing. Built so the design never changes hands between drawing and cast.

01
Design & Modelling

From Brief to 3D Model

Send a sketch, a reference, or a brief. We turn it into a high-resolution 3D surface model — texture and depth standard fabricators can't hold. Iteration is digital, so changes here cost nothing.

02
Moulding & Casting

3D-Printed Masters, Precision Casting

We 3D-print the mould master straight from the model, then vacuum-cast each panel under controlled conditions in Lumina PMAG™ or Lumina PUCOMP™, carrying every detail of the mould surface into the panel — every batch checked for surface integrity before it leaves the floor.

03
Finishing & Delivery

Artisan Finish and Crating

Each panel is finished by hand in artisan finishes, in any RAL or custom colour, then crated with its technical data sheet and installation drawing.

Materials

Two Materials,
One Standard.

One surface quality, two materials — so the same design works whether it's a dry lobby wall or a wet, curved, outdoor feature. We choose (or formulate) the right one for your context and hand it over with its technical data sheet for the specification.

Lumina PMAG™

Interior Wall & Ceiling Panels
  • Ceramic-smooth, moisture-resistant
  • Any RAL or custom colour

Lumina PUCOMP™

Curves, Exteriors & Wet Areas
  • Exterior & wet-area rated
  • Thin profiles, 8–25 mm
  • Holds the deepest texture detail

    Frequently Asked

    What to Know
    Before You Specify.

    What are your lead times?

    It depends on the project's scale, the number of moulds and the finish. We confirm the exact programme in writing in the quote.

    Lead Times →
    How heavy are the panels — do they need special support?

    Weight depends on the material and panel thickness; most applications use standard mounting systems. Structural data is included in the specification package.

    Weight & Structural →
    Is there a minimum project size?

    We work from a single feature wall to full-floor cladding. For very small jobs we assess fit together first.

    What do you provide for the specification?

    A technical data sheet and an installation drawing for every project; we settle the specification wording with your office.

    Specification Guide →
    How different is this to specify and install?

    Not very. If your practice already specifies GRC or GRG, you specify Lumina PMAG™ the same way — the same kind of fixings, the same substrate detailing. Our specification guide gives you clauses and fixing details to drop straight into your tender.

    Specification Guide →
    Do you handle installation?

    Every panel ships with installation drawings and details for your contractor. Where you need us on site, we quote installation per project. Panels are supplied EXW Sapanca by default.
