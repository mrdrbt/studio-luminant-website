Antiphon

We drew vertical bands whose two edges wave against each other, so each band narrows and widens into a chain of almond shapes; where one band narrows, its neighbour widens.

The pattern

An antiphon is a chant sung by two choirs in answer to each other. The surface works the same way: the two edges of each band curve in opposite directions, so the band pinches in one place and swells in the next. The neighbouring band follows half a step behind, widening where the first one narrows.

The relief reaches 15 mm. The middle of each almond is the highest point of the surface and the grooves where bands meet are the deepest; every transition is a soft curve, never a hard edge.

How it reads in light

With the light at the side, one flank of each almond catches it and the other falls into shadow, and the wall reads as a vertical pulse. On the wall

Antiphon is designed as a 600 × 600 mm panel with its bands upright. Laid side by side and stacked, the bands run across the panel edges unbroken, passing from one panel into the next, and the wall reads as a single surface. Where the pattern is cut at the edges of the wall is set out on the layout drawing.

Where it works

Antiphon’s upright bands set a vertical rhythm that shows more strongly on a tall wall. In raking light one flank of each almond is lit and the other falls into shadow, and the wall reads as a vertical pulse. In head-on light the shadows shorten and the surface softens into a gentle wave. Pieces come paint-ready or with an artisan finish; a paint-ready surface can be painted on site to match the room.

Frequently asked questions

What size are Antiphon panels, and how are they laid?

Antiphon is designed as a 600 × 600 mm relief wall panel, with the bands standing vertical. Laid side by side and one above another, the bands run continuous across the panel joints and the wall reads as one surface. The layout drawing sets where the pattern is cut at the edges of the wall.

How deep is the Antiphon relief?

From the crown of each almond down to the grooves where the bands meet, the relief is 15 mm deep. The transitions are soft curves rather than sharp edges.

What is the lead time for Antiphon, and how is it fixed?

Lead time is 4 weeks after contract and advance for orders up to 50 m²; for larger orders the quote sets the date. Delivery is ex-works Sapanca (EXW). Pieces are hand-cast in Lumina PMAG™ — Polymer Modified Composite Gypsum and fixed to the wall with a polymer adhesive, or with a rail system on metal stud and plasterboard walls. Pieces that wrap a column, turn a corner or follow a curved wall are cast as Bespoke, in the same motif. The minimum order is 5 m². The mould is made for your order and you approve the master before casting; both are included in the 4 weeks. The bottom course sits on a fixed ledger and each course stands on the one below; the wall surface is checked before fixing.
