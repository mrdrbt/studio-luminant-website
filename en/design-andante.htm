Andante

We drew a field of horizontal ribs and pulled it up or down along four vertical lines, the way a comb is drawn through marbling. Along each line the ribs turn in a sharp cusp, and the lines move across the wall at an uneven step.

The pattern

Andante marks music to be played at a walking pace. The wall moves the same way: the vertical lines are not evenly spaced but sit 280 to 330 mm apart, and that uneven step repeats every 1200 mm.

Each rib, on a 30 mm pitch, rises softly from a low foot to a rounded lip, then drops back in a sheer step. Along each line the ribs are pulled the same way, up or down, and turn in a sharp cusp on the line itself.

How it reads in light

On the image at the top of the page the light follows your cursor on a computer and moves as you scroll on a phone. The relief is only 7 mm, but the lip of every rib throws a fine shadow line onto the foot of the rib below. With the light high and to the side, the wall reads as a dense texture of curving lines; as it turns head-on the lines thin and the surface quietens.

On the wall

Andante is designed as a 1200 × 600 mm panel, and every panel is laid the same way up, never turned: the direction of the ribs is part of the pattern. Ribs and cusps run across the panel edges unbroken, passing from one panel into the next, and the wall reads as a single surface. Where the pattern is cut at the edges of the wall is set out on the layout drawing.

Where it works

Andante is strongest on a wall that takes its light from the side, from a window alongside or from wall-grazing lights at the ceiling. The relief is shallow, so it needs light crossing it from above and to the side; in that light the ribs, on a 30 mm pitch, read as a dense texture. In head-on light the lines thin and the surface quietens. Pieces come paint-ready and can be painted on site to match the room.

Frequently asked questions

What size are Andante panels, and how are they laid?

Andante is designed as a 1200 × 600 mm relief wall panel. Every panel is laid the same way up, never turned, because the direction of the ribs is part of the pattern; the ribs run continuous across the panel joints. The vertical lines run at 280 to 330 mm centres, an irregular pitch that repeats every 1200 mm. The layout drawing sets where the pattern is cut at the edges of the wall.

How deep is the Andante relief?

The relief is 7 mm, in ribs on a 30 mm pitch. Shallow as it is, the lip of each rib throws a fine shadow line onto the foot of the rib below, and those shadows are what make the wall read as a dense texture in raking light.

What is the lead time for Andante, and how is it fixed?

Lead time is 4 weeks after contract and advance for orders up to 50 m²; for larger orders the quote sets the date. Delivery is ex-works Sapanca (EXW). Pieces are hand-cast in Lumina PMAG™ — Polymer Modified Composite Gypsum and fixed to the wall with a polymer adhesive, or with a rail system on metal stud and plasterboard walls. Pieces that wrap a column, turn a corner or follow a curved wall are cast as Bespoke, in the same motif. The minimum order is 7.5 m². The mould is made for your order and you approve the master before casting; both are included in the 4 weeks.
