Accelerando We drew a field of cells whose size grades across the wall. View → Antiphon We drew vertical bands whose two edges wave against each other, so each band narrows and widens into a chain of almond shapes; where one band narrows, its neighbour widens. View → Cantilena The cypress, tulip, carnation and rose of the Iznik tile tradition, composed as a single panel with no repeat. View → Fioritura A floral field we drew in the vocabulary of Ottoman tile ornament: tulip buds, rosettes and serrated leaves, linked by winding stems. View → Fugato We drew a profile of coves and flat bands and divided it across three moulds. View → Glissando A surface of drifting fibres. View → Gruppetto One fan-shaped form, designed by us and laid in long S-curves. View → Hemiola A wall of blocks, each with an opening turned off square, drawn to read as hand-set. View → Melisma A maze of fine lines that we let run the length of the wall as one current. View → Ricercar A lattice built from the Gothic quatrefoil.
