See each design on its own page: on the wall, close up and in its drawing. The mould is made for your order, and you approve the master before anything is cast.

Accelerando We drew a field of cells whose size grades across the wall. Cantilena The cypress, tulip, carnation and rose of the Iznik tile tradition, composed as a single panel with no repeat. Glissando A surface of drifting fibres. Gruppetto One fan-shaped form, designed by us and laid in long S-curves. Hemiola A wall of blocks, each with an opening turned off square, drawn to read as hand-set. Melisma A maze of fine lines that we let run the length of the wall as one current. Ricercar A lattice built from the Gothic quatrefoil. Rondeau One round form, drawn once and laid as a field.

If the wall starts from your drawing, that is Bespoke: we cast it the way you drew it.
