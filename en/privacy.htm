2. What personal data we process

a) When you complete the contact form

Name and surname, email address, telephone number, company or practice name, the project information you provide (scale, location, timeline, finish preference and similar), the submission date and the source page.

b) When you email or telephone us directly

Your contact details and the content of the correspondence.

c) When you visit the site

Technical data collected through cookies — IP address, browser and device information, pages visited, time spent on the site, and the link you arrived from. Which of these depend on your consent is set out in our Cookie Policy.

Special categories of data

Studio Luminant does not collect special categories of personal data (health, biometric data, religion, membership information and similar) through this website. Please do not enter such information into our forms.

3. Purposes of processing

  • To evaluate your project enquiry and respond to you technically and commercially
  • To prepare feasibility assessments, material proposals, preliminary sketches and quotations
  • To conduct pre-contractual discussions and — where a contract is concluded — the production and delivery process
  • To retain enquiry and correspondence records and to rely on them as evidence in any dispute
  • To operate the website, maintain its security and prevent misuse
  • Where you consent, to produce statistics on how the site is used and to measure our promotional activity
  • Where you separately consent, to send you promotional material, catalogues, product announcements and event invitations

8. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

In relation to your personal data, you have the right to:

  • learn whether it is being processed and, if so, request information about that processing,
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used in line with that purpose,
  • know the third parties to whom it is transferred, in Türkiye or abroad,
  • request correction where it is incomplete or inaccurate,
  • request erasure or destruction where the conditions are met,
  • request that correction, erasure and destruction be notified to third parties to whom the data was transferred,
  • object to a result against you arising from analysis by exclusively automated systems,
  • claim compensation where you suffer loss due to unlawful processing.

9. Making a request

You may submit your requests, together with information verifying your identity:

  • to info@studioluminant.com.tr, from the email address you previously gave us and which is registered in our systems,
  • or by signed written petition to Ortabayır Mah. Talatpaşa Cad. No: 21, İç Kapı No: 501, Kağıthane / İstanbul, Türkiye.

Your request will be concluded free of charge within 30 days at the latest, depending on its nature; where the process incurs an additional cost, the fee set out in the Board’s tariff may be charged. If your request is refused or goes unanswered, you retain the right to complain to the Personal Data Protection Board.
