Unified Design System

Your Concept. Every Surface.

Specify one relief design — we produce it across walls, ceilings, columns, and feature elements. Two material systems, one consistent design language. The studies below are examples — the project is your own design. Brief us on it.

PMAG Specs →

The design studies are not a catalogue. Each one tests how far cast mineral relief can be pushed in a particular design register — geometric repeat, organic surface, Anatolian motif, and quiet texture fields that read as ground rather than pattern.

The Library is for projects that would rather start from one of our systems; most projects arrive with their own design. A drawing, a motif, a reference image, or a sketch on a napkin becomes a digital master model; a silicone mould is produced from that master; the panel is cast and hand-finished in Sapanca.

Read the studies below as reference points rather than products. Any one of them can be cast as it stands, rebuilt at a different scale or relief depth, or used only to establish a direction. The specification decision sits with the project.

Because walls, ceilings, column cladding and feature elements can all be produced from a single master model, one design language holds across every surface in a scheme.
