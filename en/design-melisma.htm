In a room

Where it works

Melisma’s maze reads across the whole wall rather than in any single piece, its fine lines running the length of the wall as one current. In all four renders on this page it covers a wall from end to end: behind the seating and reception desk of a hotel lobby, behind the sofa of a living room, along the tables of a restaurant and behind the daybeds of a spa. Each render paints the wall a different colour, and the fine lines come forward in raking light. How a lobby wall is planned around its light is covered in the hotel lobby feature wall guide. Pieces come paint-ready or with an artisan finish; a paint-ready surface can be painted on site to match the room.

Frequently asked questions

What pieces make up Melisma, and how are they laid?

Melisma is a relief wall panel design laid from pieces cast in two moulds, A and B. The letters show where each piece sits in the lay drawn on this page. Send the dimensions of your wall and we show how Melisma sits on it in a layout drawing; the edges of the wall, column faces and outside corners are worked out on the same drawing.

How deep is the Melisma relief?

The relief is 18 mm. The mould is made for your order and you approve the master before anything is cast; the cast pieces match that master, so you see the depth of the relief on it before casting starts.

What is the lead time for Melisma, and how is it fixed?

Lead time is 4 weeks after contract and advance for orders up to 50 m²; for larger orders the quote sets the date. Delivery is ex-works Sapanca (EXW). Pieces are hand-cast in Lumina PMAG™ — Polymer Modified Composite Gypsum and fixed to the wall with a polymer adhesive, or with a rail system on metal stud and plasterboard walls. Pieces that wrap a column, turn a corner or follow a curved wall are cast as Bespoke, in the same motif. The minimum order is 5 m². The mould is made for your order and you approve the master before casting; both are included in the 4 weeks. The bottom course sits on a fixed ledger and each course stands on the one below; the wall surface is checked before fixing.
