See your wall before any quote

In the online composer you choose a design and a colour, set the layout and enter your wall’s width and height. It draws the wall with the cut pieces at its edges and counts the modules you need.

The light view shows the wall’s real geometry in the city, facing and hour you pick. Each composition is saved as one code: send it to us on WhatsApp or Instagram and we are both looking at the same wall.

Single designs and modular systems

The range has two kinds of design. A single design carries one look across the whole surface. A modular system is one or a few moulds plus plain modules: turn the pieces or rearrange them, and the same set builds different walls.

Tile-sized, cast-surfaced

Here the pattern is not printed or glazed on; it is the cast surface itself. Side light throws a shadow, and the wall changes as the light moves. Designs are drawn to run on across the modules, so the wall reads as one surface rather than a grid of tiles.
